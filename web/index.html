      Accounts
      
        
      An admin can rename an account, set a new password, disable it, or delete it. The daily limit counts what an account used today and resets at 00:00 UTC; a key can have its own cap, which does not reset, and the account limit applies on top of it. Disabling signs the account out and its keys stop working, and deleting takes the account, its keys and everything recorded for it. The account you are signed in with cannot be deleted.
      
        
      
          Self-registration
          
      Closed as soon as the first account exists. While it is open, anyone can register; the first account to register is the admin.
